Your data, plainly.

A secure, first-party cookie identifies this browser for 30 days and controls access to its captures. Capture access expires after 24 hours. We use a daily keyed hash of your network address for limits; the raw address is not stored in our application database.

We count captures, outcomes, repeat-browser activity, and download requests. These metrics contain no target URLs or images. Browser activity is kept for up to 30 days after use and daily totals for 90 days. Operational job records contain the submitted URL until cleanup. Cloudflare also processes network requests and keeps service logs under its own retention.

The extension remains a separate, local tool. Its screenshots stay on your device.
