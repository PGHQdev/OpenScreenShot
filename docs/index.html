How it works

From page to finished screenshot in seconds.

Three steps, zero detours. No tab-juggling, no separate editor app.

1

Capture precisely

Full page, visible area, or any region — one click in the popup, or a keyboard shortcut. Number keys 1–3 work too.

2

Edit immediately

Every capture opens in a full-screen editor. Annotate, highlight, number steps, blur secrets, and crop — with undo all the way back.

3

Export anywhere

PNG, JPEG, WebP, or multi-page PDF with page size and margins. Download it, or copy straight to the clipboard.

Your screenshots never leave your browser.

OpenScreenShot processes everything locally on your device. No servers, no uploads. You stay in control.

  • All image data stays on your device — captures live in local extension storage until you export them.
  • No collection, no analytics, no tracking — the extension makes no network requests.
  • Zero host permissions — it cannot read any site in the background, only the tab you capture, when you capture it.
