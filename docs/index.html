Capture, edit, export.

One extension carries a screenshot from capture to finished image.

Full-page capture to export, recorded at 2.4× speed.

Capture

Full page, visible area, or a dragged region. Start from the popup, a keyboard shortcut, or the right-click menu.

Edit

Arrows, text, numbered steps, blur, spotlight, and crop, with undo all the way back.

Export

Download PNG, JPEG, WebP, or multi-page PDF, or copy it straight to the clipboard.

Everything stays on your device.

Screenshots are often the most sensitive images on your machine. The extension makes no network requests at all.

  • Captures and recordings live in local browser storage, on your machine, until you export or delete them.
  • Host permissions are empty. The extension reads a tab only when you capture it, and cross-site recording is a separate opt-in.
  • MIT licensed, with no watermarks. Every line of code is on GitHub.

"permissions": [
  "activeTab",
  "scripting",
  "storage",
  "unlimitedStorage",
  "downloads",
  "contextMenus",
  "clipboardWrite",
  "offscreen"
],
"host_permissions": [],
"optional_permissions": ["tabCapture"],
"optional_host_permissions": ["<all_urls>"]
activeTabaccess the current tab, only when you click
scriptingrun the capture script, and the recording overlay, on that tab
storagekeep your settings and last capture on-device
downloadssave exports to your Downloads folder
contextMenusadd one capture submenu to the right-click menu
clipboardWritecopy a screenshot; it never reads the clipboard
offscreenrun the recording engine in a hidden document
host_permissions: []no standing access to any website
tabCaptureoptional; requested once, at your first recording
<all_urls>optional host permission, only if you turn on "Record across sites"
